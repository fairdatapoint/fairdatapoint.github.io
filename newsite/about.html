About the FAIR Data Point

The FAIR Data Point was developed to address the growing need for a simple, yet effective, way to make (meta)data truly FAIR. The FAIR Data Point (sometimes abbreviated to FDP) is the realisation of the vision of a group of authors of the original paper on FAIR on how (meta)data could be presented on the web using existing standards, and without the need of custom APIs.

A FAIR Data Point ultimately stores information about data sets (and other digital objects). And just like the webserver in the WWW in the beginning of the 1990s brought the power of publishing text to anyone, a FDP aims to give anyone the power of putting their own metadata on the web.

The system is called a FAIR Data Point because it takes care of a lot of the issues that need to be taken care of to make data FAIR; especially with the metadata needed for Findability and Reusability, and a uniform open way of Accessing the data. The FAIR Data Point also addresses the Interoperability of the metadata it stores, but it leaves the Interoperability aspects for the data itself to the data provider.

Our team is composed of passionate researchers and developers from various institutions (University of Twente, Leiden University Medical Center, Universidad Politecnica de Madrid) committed to open science, data-sharing and the FAIR principles. We believe that by providing a straightforward tool, we can empower the global community to make their data more findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable, fostering collaboration and innovation.
